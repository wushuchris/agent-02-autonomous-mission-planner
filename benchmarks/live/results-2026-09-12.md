## Interpretation

This run demonstrates live provider connectivity, structured-output compatibility, first-pass contract reliability, resource-label discipline, missing-information surfacing, and preservation of the tested approval contract across the eight controlled cases.

Because all eight first proposals passed deterministic validation, the live run did **not** exercise the bounded replan repair path. That path is covered by deterministic and mocked-model regression tests, but a live repair rate cannot be claimed from this run.

Human usefulness fields remain intentionally unscored. The benchmark does not establish operational search-and-rescue safety, factual completeness, aviation compliance, navigation quality, broad jailbreak resistance, run-to-run stability, or business ROI. One execution per scenario is an initial evidence layer rather than a variance study.
